Booking requirements

Everything you need before you book

Renting through RideNest is quick and secure. Make sure you have these essentials ready before your journey begins.

Valid Driving Licence

A valid Nepali driving licence or an accepted International Driving Permit is required.

Minimum Age

Drivers must be at least 21 years old for most vehicles. Premium vehicles may have higher age requirements.

Identity Verification

Complete your RideNest profile verification to enjoy faster booking approvals and a trusted rental experience.

Security Deposit

Selected vehicles require a refundable security deposit before pickup. The amount varies by vehicle.

Need help before booking?

Our support team is here to guide you through verification, payments, pickup, and everything you need for a smooth rental experience.
